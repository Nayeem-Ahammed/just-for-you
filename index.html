The defining chapters that brought two separate lives together into one beautiful forever.

Autumn 2019 · College Life

The Beginning ("How We Met")

We were school friends first—just two people sharing the same little world, never knowing what our story would become.

Then school ended, and life took us to different colleges. Somewhere between growing up and growing apart, I realized that my feelings for you had quietly become something more.

Distance separated our days, but somehow, it brought my heart closer to yours.

Some love stories begin with a friendship; ours began before we even knew it was love.

💍
2022 - 2024 · Mymensingh - Cumilla

The Proposal ("Saying Yes")

I couldn't keep my feelings hidden anymore, so I proposed to you over the phone.

You didn't say yes.
You simply said, “We're good friends.” I understood that you think about your familly.

But I stayed. We kept talking, kept sharing our days, and slowly, somewhere between all those conversations, your heart found its way to mine.

Then, in 2023, you finally said the word I had been waiting for — YES.

I asked for your heart once; you gave it to me after making me wait for it.

☕
19 October 2023 · Zainul Abedin Park, Mymensingh

First Date ("Where Officially Met")

After so many calls, messages, and countless conversations, we finally met in person on 19 October 2023.

It was our first meeting—not the beginning of our story, but the moment our story finally stepped out of the phone and into the real world.

From that day, our long-distance journey continued. Different places, busy days, and miles between us — but always the same two hearts choosing each other.

After countless words through a screen, I finally got to hear your laughter beside me.

👰
365 Days Ago · Shahgonj, Gouripur, Mymensingh

Our Wedding Day ("Becoming Us")

And then came the hardest, bravest, and most beautiful chapter of our story.

On 3 October 2025, we finally became husband and wife.


But I know something that I will never forget— you fought harder for us than I ever could. You carried the weight of difficult conversations. You faced your family. You managed the fears, questions, emotions, and everything that stood between us and this day.

While I was dreaming about our future, you were fighting to make that future possible. You didn't just say you loved me.

You proved it.

You stood for us when it wasn't easy. You held onto our relationship when things could have been much simpler if you had let go.

So when I look at our wedding day, I don't just see the day I became your husband. I see the day your courage, patience, love, and countless efforts finally brought us here. If our marriage has a beginning written in a single word, it is us. But if there is someone who deserves the loudest credit for making that us. possible—
it's you.

I may call you my wife, but I will always remember that you were the one who fought hardest to make us one.
And for everything you did—for every difficult conversation, every tear, every moment you chose us—

thank you, my love.
You didn't just walk beside me to our wedding day; you carried us there.
